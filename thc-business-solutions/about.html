We Didn't Start as Marketers. That's the Point.

THC Business Solutions was built by people who worked inside healthcare operations, not inside advertising — which changes what you notice is broken, and in what order you'd fix it.

Our Purpose

Why THC Exists

Most consultancies and agencies serving healthcare organizations are built around what's visible: campaigns, rebrands, training days. They're good at producing those things. What they're not built to do is notice that a rebrand is being asked to compensate for a scheduling system nobody has fixed, or that a training day is being used as a substitute for changing how decisions actually get made on the floor.

THC Business Solutions was built specifically to catch what gets missed in that gap — not as an add-on to a standard consulting practice, but as the starting premise of the whole company.

Our Method

The Method Behind the Diagnosis

Every engagement — regardless of which of the three verticals it starts in — runs through the same three-part method. It's not a formality; it's the reason a THC engagement tends to hold after the invoice is paid, where a single-layer fix often doesn't.

Human & Behavioral Diagnostics

Before anything structural gets recommended, we spend time understanding how people actually behave inside the organization as it currently exists: what a nurse has learned happens when she flags a scheduling conflict, whether a front-desk coordinator has any real authority to fix a recurring problem or just absorbs the complaints about it, and where the gap sits between what a policy says and what actually happens on a Tuesday afternoon.

Analytical & Data-Driven Rigor

In parallel, we run a direct, numbers-based analysis of workflows, cost structures, staffing patterns, and supply data. This is built on a proprietary four-layer business analysis framework, developed from cross-industry case work and adapted here to the specific operating realities of healthcare.

Business Strategy & Execution

The two diagnostic layers converge into a single sequenced roadmap. We're specific about the word sequenced: a healthcare organization can usually absorb only so much change at once, and the order interventions happen in is often the difference between a fix that holds and one that gets quietly reversed.

Founding

Who's Behind It

THC Business Solutions was founded by a biomedical engineer with a background in healthcare business operations — not by a marketing executive or a management consultant by trade. That background shapes how the company approaches a client engagement: reading a workflow, a piece of clinical equipment, or a procurement contract the way an engineer reads a system, for how the parts actually interact, rather than the way a brand strategist reads a market.

Beliefs

What We Believe

Diagnosis before treatment

We don't recommend a fix before we've confirmed what's actually broken. That sounds obvious; it's also the single most skipped step in healthcare consulting and branding.

Healthcare, deliberately, for now

Our diagnostic framework was built from cross-industry case work, but our practice is deliberately healthcare-only in this phase. The regulatory environment and clinical stakes are different enough that we'd rather go deep here first.

Evidence over adjectives

We'd rather tell you what a workflow audit found than tell you we're "innovative." If a claim in our materials can't be traced back to something we actually did, it doesn't belong in them.
